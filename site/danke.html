Your first start

  1. Start the setupOpen StemMaker-Setup.exe. No admin rights needed.
  2. Unzip the ZIPUnzip StemMaker.zip to any folder and start StemMaker.exe. No installation needed.
  3. Windows warning?If Windows shows “Windows protected your PC”, click “More info” and then “Run anyway”.
  4. Drag your music inFiles or whole folders, then click Start. Each track becomes Title.stem.mp4 for Traktor and Mixxx.
